# Support

Thanks for taking a look at Looks. We are glad the code is useful.

It is shared as-is under the MIT license ([`LICENSE`](./LICENSE)), and it is not covered by commercetools support. We can't promise replies, fixes or a timeline. Issues and pull requests are still welcome, and we will look at them when we can.

What helps most:

- **Adapt it.** The license allows you to fork, change and reuse the code. Check it against your own project, scopes and limits before you rely on it.
- **Report a problem clearly.** A short description and the steps to reproduce it help other readers, even if nobody gets to it straight away.
- **Use commercetools support for the platform.** Questions about Projects, the Merchant Center or Connect go to commercetools support through your usual channel.
